About UrbanBrew

A simple website for a Business Analytics project.

This site demonstrates how a small business website can be connected to Google Analytics 4.

Project Purpose

The website contains multiple pages so Google Analytics can collect page views, visitors, engagement, and navigation behavior.

What can be measured?

  • Active users and new users
  • Page views
  • Engagement time
  • Traffic sources
  • Browser and device information
  • Custom button click events
